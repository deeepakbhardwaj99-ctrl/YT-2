# Voice and Delivery Plan — Trojan War

**Status:** Narrator selected; four additional master-brief auditions pending user selections.

## Locked delivery decision

The story will be produced in **four parts** at a **132-wpm average narrator pace**. This pace is calm enough for mythological material, allows compassionate pauses around loss, and keeps the assembled runtime close to 25 minutes without rushing the evidence section.

| Part | Story coverage | Spoken VO estimate | Finished-part target |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 1 — The Spark | Cold Open; Chapters 1–3 | 6:55 | ~7:10 |
| 2 — The Cost of Glory | Chapters 4–7 | 6:14 | ~6:30 |
| 3 — Prophecies and the Horse | Chapters 8–10 | 6:17 | ~6:35 |
| 4 — Fire, Homecoming, Evidence | Chapters 11–13 | 4:46 | ~5:10 |
| **Complete film** | **Cold Open; Chapters 1–13** | **24:13** | **~25:25** |

Finished-part targets include title/tease cards, calm transitions, and the final 7–10-second reflective dissolve. All clip boundaries will later be recut against measured VO durations.

## Character-voice decision

The script includes more than thirty named speaking roles. The master brief explicitly permits **the narrator, up to three extra character voices, and a distinct mascot voice**. To follow that requirement faithfully—and to avoid a chaotic, inconsistent soundscape—the film will use **five consistent voices**, not thirty separate synthetic voices.

Each named character still receives a distinct performance profile through line delivery, pause length, level, and visual lip-sync; no dialogue tag will be read aloud. A character never changes assigned voice during the production.

| Track | Intended role coverage | Performance direction | Selection status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Narrator | All plain-paragraph narration | Mature, warm, low-mid masculine English; calm, compassionate, credible; firmer in the final act | **Selected: `voice-00`** |
| Mascot | Meno's short interjections | Lighter, warm, alert, gently wry—not comic relief that breaks the gravity | Audition pending |
| Dialogue A — authority / warriors | Zeus, Agamemnon, Menelaus, Achilles, Hector, Priam, Ajax, Neoptolemus, Poseidon | Grounded adult masculine, textured and controlled | Audition pending |
| Dialogue B — younger / strategic men | Paris, Odysseus, Patroclus, Palamedes, Philoctetes, Calchas, Chryses, Sinon, Helenus, Epeius and minor male lines | Clear, thoughtful adult masculine, slightly lighter and more agile than Dialogue A | Audition pending |
| Dialogue C — women / divine / prophetic | Eris, Hera, Athena, Aphrodite, Helen, Thetis, Iphigenia, Andromache, Clytemnestra, Cassandra, Oenone and minor female lines | Clear adult feminine, poised and emotionally precise; never exaggerated | Audition pending |

## Pitch policy

No artificial global pitch shifting will be applied. The voices are selected for naturally differentiated registers, which is more believable and avoids digital artifacts. Emotional strength comes from native performance, deliberate silence, music ducking, and image/motion emphasis.

## Current gate

The narrator is user-selected. The user will now audition the mascot and the three additional dialogue tracks before character-voice casting is locked.
