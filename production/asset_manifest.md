## Locked hybrid visual bridge

- **Environment treatment:** painterly-realistic, cinematic Bronze Age Aegean and Anatolian environments. Every background is empty of foreground people, letters, logos, and watermarks, with open lower-third ground for 2D characters.
- **Character treatment:** clean 2D illustrated cut-outs, confident dark outlines, simple dot eyes and minimal mouths, flat-to-soft shading, thick warm-cream sticker outline on the mascot only.
- **Shared grade:** sun-warmed parchment highlights, oxidized bronze shadows, terracotta accents, Aegean-blue distance haze, subtle unified film grain and contact shadows.
- **Overlay palette:** terracotta accent `#C8543B`; parchment `#F1E1B7`; charcoal `#17202A`; bronze `#8B5A2B`; Aegean dusk `#3E5976`.
- **Animation energy:** full but restrained—slow moving camera, deliberate eased entrances, breathing/blink idles, gentle gesture swaps, and no frantic cuts.
