# Part 3 Background Batch 02 — QC Record

**Scope:** 10 initial candidates (five for clip 03's shared-meal tent interior, five for clip 04's first-light beach camp) plus 5 targeted rerolls. All 15 images were individually opened from filename-bannered review copies; embedded filename, dimensions, top-centre RGB median and SHA-256 prefix were checked against each source. Every image was 1376×768. No candidate contained people/faces/bodies, text/letters/numbers/inscriptions, logos/watermarks or modern objects.
